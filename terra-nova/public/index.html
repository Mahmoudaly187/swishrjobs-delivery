Rules. On a 5x5 board, first to control 8 tiles wins. Each turn you collect 1 influence per owned tile (+1 per well), then take one action: claim an adjacent unowned tile for its terrain cost, build a well for 3, or raid an adjacent enemy well for 4. A well pays back in three turns; a raid erases it. If the board fills, most tiles wins.
Agents: POST /api/game then POST /api/game/{id}/move with {"token","action":{"type":"claim","x":1,"y":0}}. See /api docs in the repo README.
